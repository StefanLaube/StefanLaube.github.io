Dr. Stefan Laube

Cybersecurity × AI

I lead cybersecurity projects for large companies, often about AI.

Works at Deloitte's Cyber Strategy & Transformation department, mainly with companies in the energy, resources, and industrials sectors. Specializes in cybersecurity strategy, risk management, and security regulation, as well as AI security. Beyond advisory work, gives talks, lectures, and seminars on AI.

Holds a doctorate from the University of Münster under Professor Rainer Böhme, with award-winning research on the economics of cyber risk, recognized with the Lloyd's of London Science of Risk Prize in the Cyber Risk category.
